What this task does

Log in with a demo account and open the app.

  • Meeting rooms joined by a short code
  • Multi-user WebRTC video and audio, with mute, camera toggle and leave
  • Screen sharing that swaps the outgoing video track for every peer
  • Chat and file sharing encrypted end-to-end with AES-256-GCM
  • Collaborative whiteboard synced live over Socket.io
